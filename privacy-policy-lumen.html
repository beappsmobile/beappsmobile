Lumen — Tactical LED Flashlight

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026  |  Package: com.beappsmobile.flashlight

Privacy at a Glance

Lumen does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app operates completely offline, contains no advertisements, and includes no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal or non-personal information from users of the Lumen Flashlight application. Specifically, the app does not collect, access, or record:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising IDs
  • Location data (GPS or network-based)
  • Photos, videos, audio recordings, contacts, or files
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry, or behavioral tracking data

2. No Advertisements or Third-Party SDKs

Lumen is completely ad-free. We do not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics services, or external tracking libraries. Your usage of the app remains strictly private on your device.

3. Hardware & Device Access

To provide its core lighting features, the app interacts locally with the following standard Android system interfaces:

  • LED Flash / Torch Hardware (android.hardware.camera.flash): Used exclusively via Android's system CameraManager API to turn the rear LED torch on and off and adjust its brightness or strobe pattern. The app never opens a camera preview and never captures images or video.
  • Battery Status: Reads the device's current battery percentage locally from the Android system to display an on-screen battery indicator while the flashlight is in use. This information never leaves your device.
  • Screen Brightness Control: Used locally when "Screen Light" mode is active to illuminate your display.

4. Offline Operation & Data Security

The application does not request internet access (android.permission.INTERNET is not used). Because no data is collected or transmitted off your device, there is zero risk of your personal information being exposed, sold, or shared with third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable global privacy regulations (including GDPR).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, we will post the revised version on this page and update the "Effective Date" at the top of the document.
