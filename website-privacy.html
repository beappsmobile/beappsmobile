Website Privacy Notice

Last Updated: November 16, 2026  |  Scope: beappsmobile.com Website

Scope of This Notice

This notice applies strictly to visitors of the beappsmobile.com website. For the privacy policies governing our mobile applications, please refer to the respective App Privacy Policy links in the website footer.

1. No Tracking Cookies or Marketing Profiles

The beappsmobile.com website does not use advertising, behavioral tracking, or profiling cookies. You can browse this website and download our PC companion installers without creating an account or submitting personal data.

2. Cloudflare Hosting, Security & Traffic Analytics

This website is delivered and protected using Cloudflare, Inc. infrastructure (CDN, DDoS protection, and privacy-first Web Analytics):

  • Security & CDN Delivery (Legitimate Interest — GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): When you access this website, Cloudflare processes standard HTTP request metadata (including your IP address, browser user-agent, and requested URL) to route traffic, mitigate automated abuse, and protect the site from security threats. If Cloudflare sets a security cookie (such as __cf_bm), it is strictly necessary for bot protection and expires automatically.
  • Cookie-Free Web Analytics: Aggregate traffic metrics (such as page views, referrers, and country-level visits) are measured without cookies, without local storage, and without fingerprinting individual visitors.

3. Embedded Demo Video (YouTube Privacy-Enhanced Mode)

Our homepage features an optional walkthrough video hosted on YouTube. To protect your privacy, the video player is not loaded automatically when you visit the page. Only when you explicitly click Play Demo Video does your browser connect to youtube-nocookie.com (YouTube's Privacy-Enhanced Mode), which prevents YouTube from storing tracking cookies for personalized advertising unless you interact with the video player.

4. Direct Email Contact

If you contact us directly via support@beappsmobile.com, we will use your email address and message content solely to respond to your inquiry and provide technical support.

5. Your Rights & Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Website Privacy Notice, please contact us at support@beappsmobile.com.
