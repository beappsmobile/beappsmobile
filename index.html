Support & Troubleshooting

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common setup and connectivity questions.

How do I connect my phone to my PC over Wi-Fi?
Make sure both your smartphone and your Windows PC are connected to the same local Wi-Fi network (or mobile hotspot). Launch the PC client on your computer, open the mobile app, and either scan the displayed QR code or select your PC's IP address to connect immediately.
Can I use PC Keyboard or Presentation Remote without Wi-Fi?
Yes! Both applications support Bluetooth connectivity. Simply pair your phone with your PC in Windows Bluetooth settings, start the PC client, and select Bluetooth mode inside the mobile app.
What if Windows Firewall blocks the connection?
When you launch the PC client for the first time, Windows Defender Firewall may ask for permission to allow communication on private networks. Ensure you check Private networks and click Allow access so your phone can reach the PC server.
How can I request account or data deletion?
Most of our utility apps operate without requiring user registration. For apps that support user accounts, you can submit a deletion request anytime via our Account Deletion Request page or by emailing support@beappsmobile.com.
